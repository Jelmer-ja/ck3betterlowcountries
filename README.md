A flavor mod for the Low Countries region. The current features are as follows:

* New culture setup with two new cultures, Picard and Walloon, as well as a reworked Frisian culture. Picard and Frisian each get a new unique tradition. Picard and Walloon have their own separate name lists, though house and dynasty names will still use the French list. 
* New title localization for counties and baronies in Dutch, French, Walloon, Picard and Frisian for the Low Countries region
* A new de jure setup with Picardy as a separate duchy
* Four new realm decisions (Unite the Netherlands, Restore Old Saxony, A New Frisian Realm and Picard Nationhood).
* 12 new special buildings for the Low Countries region and northern France. Some of these are nerfed versions of buildings taken from Europa Magnifica, others are original.
* A new bookmark for 867 with a slightly revised character setup. Ubbe Ragnarsson now rules Zeeland (based on the historical theory that he was based in Walcheren before leaving to support the Great Heathen Army) and is involved in his brothers' wars against the Saxons. Gerulf has been moved to the County of Holland. Three new wanderers have been added as well, including Ragnar's missing sons Eirikr and Agnarr, the legendary giants Ellert and Brammert, and the Frisian Radboud Waldgerszoon. 

Currently in development:
* Various folklore-related events and characters
* Other bookmarks
* Balancing the various new bonuses

Most changes are of the mod's changes in the on_action script, so the mod should be compatible with almost every other mod that doesn't also change the de jure structure of France. However, the Picard unique building might not be triggered if other mods are used due to the overriding of the building list. Two important vanilla files which are overwritten are the list of possible buildings for castle holdings and the historical wars file, so the main mod conflicts will come from there.
